1GST Invoice Kya Hoti Hai?

GST invoice ek legal document hai jo ek GST-registered business apne customer ko deta hai jab koi product bechta hai ya service provide karta hai. Yeh document officially prove karta hai ki transaction hua, kitna GST charge kiya gaya, aur customer GST credit (ITC) claim kar sakta hai.

India mein GST (Goods and Services Tax) July 1, 2017 ko launch hua — VAT, Service Tax, Excise Duty sab replace karke ek unified tax system bana. Tab se har registered business ke liye apni har sale par GST invoice issue karna legally mandatory hai. Invoice nahi diya toh GST department se notice aur penalty dono aa sakti hain.

✅

2GST Invoice Kiske Liye Zaroori Hai?

GST registration threshold aur invoice requirement samajhna zaroori hai. Not everyone needs to register — but once registered, invoice compulsory hai:

  • Goods business: Annual turnover Rs 40 lakh se zyada (Special category states: Rs 20 lakh)
  • Services business: Annual turnover Rs 20 lakh se zyada (Special category: Rs 10 lakh)
  • E-commerce sellers: Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra — regardless of turnover, registration mandatory
  • Inter-state supply: Kisi bhi amount par — doosre state ko becho toh registration chahiye
  • Exporters: Export business ke liye mandatory — zero-rated supply
  • Reverse Charge: Kuch specific services receive karne par bhi invoice zaroori
💡
Voluntary Registration ka Faayda: Threshold se kam turnover hai toh bhi voluntarily register karke B2B clients ko ITC benefit de sakte hain — professional credibility badhti hai aur clients ke liye aap preferred vendor ban jaate hain.

4CGST vs SGST vs IGST — Exact Difference

Yeh sabse common confusion hai GST mein. Teen types hain aur kaunsa lagta hai woh transaction ki location par depend karta hai — aapka business kahan hai aur customer kahan hai:

Central GST
CGST
Central Government ko jaata hai. Same state transaction mein lagta hai — SGST ke saath equally split. Delhi seller → Delhi buyer = CGST + SGST.
State GST
SGST
State Government ko jaata hai. CGST ke saath same rate par. 18% GST = CGST 9% + SGST 9%. Dono alag alag line items mein invoice par show honge.
Integrated GST
IGST
Inter-state ya export transactions par lagta hai. CGST + SGST ki jagah sirf IGST — same total rate. Mumbai seller → Delhi buyer = IGST only.
🎯
Easy Rule — Yaad Rakhein:
Same state = CGST + SGST (dono half-half)
Different state = IGST (poora ek)
Export = 0% IGST (zero-rated, refund eligible)

5GST Rates Guide — Kaunse Products Par Kitna Tax?

India mein 5 main GST slabs hain — 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, aur 28%. Yeh jaanna zaroori hai ki aapka product/service kaunse slab mein aata hai:

0%
Basic food items, educational services, healthcare, books, newspapers
5%
Packaged food, transport, small restaurant food, textile fabric
12%
Processed food, computers, mobile phones, readymade garments >Rs 1000
18%
Most IT/software services, consulting, electronics, AC restaurants, hotel rooms
28%
Luxury cars, tobacco, cement, large white goods, casino, aerated drinks
⚠️
Important: GST rates change hote rehte hain — GST Council ki meetings mein revisions hoti hain. Hamesha gst.gov.in par ya apne CA se current rate confirm karein. Galat rate charge karna penalty ka kaaran ban sakta hai.

6HSN aur SAC Codes — Kaunsa Use Karein?

HSN (Harmonized System of Nomenclature) codes goods ke liye hain. SAC (Services Accounting Code) codes services ke liye. Invoice mein correct code daalna zaroori hai — turnover ke hisaab se digits ki requirement change hoti hai:

Annual TurnoverHSN Digits RequiredApplicable For
Rs 5 crore tak4-digit HSN codeMost small businesses
Rs 5 crore se Rs 50 crore6-digit HSN codeMedium businesses
Rs 50 crore se zyada8-digit HSN codeLarge businesses, exporters
Services (all turnover)6-digit SAC codeAll service providers

HSN Code kaise dhundhein: gst.gov.in → Services → Search HSN/SAC Code section par jaayein. Ya Google par "HSN code for [product name] India" search karein.

8Freelancers ke liye Special GST Guide

India mein 15 million+ freelancers hain — content writers, developers, designers, consultants. GST unke liye kaise kaam karta hai yeh differently samajhna zaroori hai:

  • Rs 20 lakh se zyada: GST registration compulsory — invoice mandatory

9Common GST Invoice Mistakes — Jinse Zaroor Bachein

Yeh mistakes practically bahut common hain — aur in mein se kuch karna serious legal problems create kar sakta hai:

  • Galat GSTIN daalna: Customer ka GSTIN carefully verify karein gst.gov.in par — galat number se unhe ITC nahi milega aur woh aapse unhappy honge
  • Invoice sequence todna: Invoice numbers sequential hone chahiye within a financial year — gaps ya random numbers = scrutiny ka risk
  • Galat tax type apply karna: CGST+SGST ki jagah IGST ya vice versa — supplier aur customer states carefully check karein
  • HSN/SAC code missing ya galat: Required turnover par HSN missing = penalty. Galat HSN code = wrong tax rate = refund issues
  • Invoice date delay karna: GST rules ke according invoice issue time limit hai — goods = delivery ke time, services = 30 days mein
  • Record nahi rakhna: GST law ke according sabhi invoices minimum 6 saal tak rakhne chahiye — digital copies organized folder mein save karo
  • GST returns file nahi karna: Invoice banane se kaam nahi hoga — GSTR-1 monthly/quarterly aur GSTR-3B monthly file karna compulsory hai
📁
Record Keeping Tip: Ek simple Google Drive folder structure banao: "Invoices → 2026-27 → April" — har mahine ki invoices alag folder mein. Month ke end mein PDF download karke save karo. Simple hai, legally compliant hai, aur CA ke kaam bhi aata hai.
